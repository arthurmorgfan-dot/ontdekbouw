# BOUW concept artwork

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this visual rebuild. These scenes are imagined possibilities, not photographs of BOUW infrastructure, programs, farms, pilots or outcomes. Visible concept captions accompany them on the website. All pre-existing project artwork remains intact.

## Saved sources

- `public/images/future/river-civilization.webp` — homepage, vision, follow and closing environmental scene.
- `public/images/future/food.webp` — the two food proposal openings.
- `public/images/future/movement.webp` — the movement proposal opening.

Responsive delivery assets live in `public/images/scenes/`. `node scripts/build-scene-images.mjs` regenerates them and `src/lib/scene-images.ts`. The source scenes are optimized as WebP at quality 85; delivery assets use quality 80 and exact width descriptors, never enlarging the sources. CSS selects deliberate focal points per project and viewport. The large environmental crops request enough resolution for their height, instead of treating a narrow crop as a small thumbnail.

## Final prompt set

### River civilization

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic environmental artwork for BOUW website, wide landscape 3:2 image, no typography. Create an extraordinarily beautiful believable possible civilization integrated into monumental nature at sunrise. Elevated aerial view over a broad winding river estuary, marshland, forests and low mist, with a quietly advanced compact settlement on the right bank: low terraced stone and timber buildings, understated green roofs, tiny bridges, a few cultivated fields, and warm windows. The landscape must dwarf the settlement. The top left third is dark atmospheric forest and open charcoal sky, calm negative space for a large website heading. Warm natural sunlight emerges on the far right horizon. Deep near-black forest, muted mineral greens, bronze water, cream light. Photorealistic architectural concept art, serious timeless fine art landscape photography, cinematic atmospheric perspective. Future feels enormous but geographically plausible on Earth. Emotional awe of Pandora, never literal: no alien plants, floating mountains, blue people, fantasy structures, skyscrapers, neon, glowing interfaces or science fiction vehicles. No text, logos, labels, statistics, collage or webpage. Subtle technology subordinate to landscape. Conceptual scene, not an existing construction.

### food

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic editorial artwork for a BOUW research proposal about affordable fresh food, portrait 2:3. Quiet beautiful possible future on Earth, an understated timber and glass greenhouse in a broad Northern European river landscape, green cultivation beds with fresh vegetables in the foreground, a simple crate of produce near a worktable, broad fertile fields and a small compact human settlement visible beyond the glass. Landscape and natural light dominate, subtle plausible technology. Photorealistic architectural concept photography, warm morning light, deep forest green shadows, amber sunlight and stone neutral palette. Generous composition with strong sense of distance and believable human scale, premium and serious. No charts, typography, logos, signs, futuristic neon, sci-fi interface, alien plants, fantasy, no claim of an existing farm. A hypothetical scene to illustrate a research idea.

### movement

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic editorial artwork for a BOUW research proposal about daily movement, portrait 2:3. A possible future Northern European public landscape at warm dawn: a wide riverside meadow, mature trees and a modest stone and timber town far away, with a simple walking and running path winding through the greenery and a few very small anonymous human figures naturally walking or jogging. Enormous landscape, quiet civilization, no monumental invented structures. Photorealistic environmental concept photography, warm low natural light through mist, muted dark green trees, bronze and warm cream highlights. Intentional portrait composition with path inviting viewer forward, scale and possibility. No close-up faces, no fitness branding, no event, no identifiable people, no text, signs, dashboards, icons, neon, aliens or fantasy. A hypothetical scene illustrating a research idea, not a real BOUW program.
